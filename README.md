## 🚀 How to Host on Cloudflare Pages (Free)
1. Visit the [Cloudflare Dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) &rarr; **Workers & Pages**.
2. Click **Create application** &rarr; **Pages** &rarr; **Upload assets**.
3. Choose a project name (e.g. `barabelun-budo-academy`).
4. Drag and drop the `barabelun-budo-academy` directory.
5. Click **Deploy**. Your site will be online instantly worldwide with a free `.pages.dev` subdomain and SSL certificate.
